## 1. Getting started

### On a computer

Open **https://anthropic-arena.web.app** in any modern browser. Nothing to
install.

**Tip — install it as an app.** In Chrome or Edge, an install icon appears in
the address bar. Click it and Anthropic Arena opens in its own window, works
offline, and gets a desktop shortcut.

### On Android

Either open the web address above and tap **Install** when prompted, or install
the release APK supplied by your team.

### On iPhone / iPad

Open the web address in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

> On iOS the app runs in local-only mode: your progress is saved on the device,
> but you won't appear on the shared leaderboard.

### Works offline

Once loaded, the app works without a connection. Every question is stored inside
the app. Your progress saves on your device and syncs when you're back online.

## 2. Signing in

Five ways in:

| Option | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Microsoft** | Colleagues at work | Uses your existing work account. **Recommended.** |
| **Google** | Personal use on web | Web only |
| **Email & password** | Anyone | Create a password on first use |
| **Guest** | A quick look | ⚠️ See the limits below |
| **Apple** | Demo only | Not a full sign-in yet |

### Guest mode limits

Guest sign-in is one tap and lets you try the learning pillar straight away.
But guests **cannot**:

- ❌ Open the **Certifications** tab — it stays locked
- ❌ Appear on the **leaderboard**
- ❌ Sync progress to another device

### Upgrading from guest

**You will not lose your progress.** Go to **Profile → Sign out**, then sign in
with Microsoft, Google or email. Your XP, streak and stars carry over to the
new account.

## 4. Learning: courses, levels and quizzes

### The Learn tab

At the top you'll find:

- **Daily goal ring** — fills as you earn XP. The target is **100 XP a day**.
- **Continue card** — jumps straight back to where you left off.
- **Course list** — six courses, 42 levels, 294 questions in total.

### The level map

Tap a course to open its level map — a winding path of level nodes.

| Node appearance | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Lit up | Unlocked — ready to play |
| Dimmed with a lock | Locked — pass the previous level first |
| Showing 1–3 stars | Completed, with your best result |

**Levels unlock in order.** You can't skip ahead, so foundations get covered.

### Playing a quiz

Each question shows four options. Tap one to select it.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Next** | Confirm and move on |
| **Previous** | Go back and change an earlier answer |
| **Skip** | Come back to it later |
| **Get unstuck** | Opens a resource link for that topic |

Question order is **shuffled every attempt**, so replaying isn't memorising a
sequence.

### After the quiz

You'll see your score, stars earned, and XP gained — plus a **full review**:
every question, what you answered, the correct answer, and an explanation.

> **Read the review.** It's where the actual learning happens.

**Replay any level any time** to improve your stars. Your best result is kept.

## 5. Certifications: practice exams

> 🔒 **Sign-in required.** Guests see a sign-in prompt instead of the catalogue.

### Choosing a credential

Credentials are grouped by category — Administrator & App Builder, Architect,
Artificial Intelligence, Associate / Foundations, Consultant, Developer,
Marketing.

**41 credentials are listed. 8 currently have question banks** (395 questions).
The rest show **Coming soon**. Retired credentials are listed but can't be
taken.

Credentials with banks today:

| Credential | Questions |
|---|---|
| Platform Developer I | 195 |
| Platform Administrator | 150 |
| Platform Developer II | 10 |
| Agentforce Specialist | 8 |
| CPQ Specialist | 8 |
| Data Cloud (Data 360) Consultant | 8 |
| Sales Cloud Consultant | 8 |
| Service Cloud Consultant | 8 |

### Choosing an exam set

Some credentials offer more than one set — for example **Spring 26 Pattern**.
If there's only one, the exam starts immediately.

### Taking the exam

The exam mirrors the real thing:

- **Official pass mark, time limit and question count** for that credential
- **Live countdown** in the header
- **⏰ Auto-submit at 0:00** — whatever you've answered is scored
- **Question palette** — jump to any question, see what's still unanswered
- **Submit** manually whenever you're ready

Questions are drawn **at random** from the bank each attempt, so no two runs are
identical.

### Multi-select questions ⚠️

Some questions expect **more than one answer** — they'll say so.

> **These are scored all-or-nothing.** Every correct option must be ticked, and
> no incorrect ones. Three right out of four scores **zero**.
>
> This is exactly how the real Salesforce exam scores. It's the single most
> common reason people fail.

### Your result

You'll see your score, pass or fail against the official mark, a **per-topic
breakdown**, and a full review of every question.

**The per-topic breakdown is the most useful part.** It tells you which subject
areas to revise instead of re-reading everything.

## 6. Ranking

Two leaderboards, switchable at the top:

| Scope | Ranks by |
|---|---|
| **All-time** | Total XP ever earned |
| **Weekly** | XP earned this week — resets Monday |

The top three appear on a podium; everyone else in a list. **Your own row is
always highlighted**, wherever you are in the table.

**Notes**

- Guests don't appear — sign in with a real account to compete
- Players with no score are hidden
- The board shows the top 50

## 9. How scoring works

### XP

| Action | XP |
|---|---|
| Each correct answer | Base XP |
| Passing a level | **+25 bonus** |
| A perfect run | **+50 extra bonus** |
| Daily goal | 100 XP |

### Stars

| Stars | Requires |
|---|---|
| ☆☆☆ | Below the pass mark |
| ★☆☆ | Reaching the pass mark |
| ★★☆ | 85% or above |
| ★★★ | **100% — a perfect run** |

### Streaks

Complete **at least one level a day** to keep your streak alive. Miss a day and
it resets to zero.

### Badges

Ten badges for milestones — first level, streak lengths, perfect runs,
completing every level in a course, and more. Locked badges show how close you
are.

## 10. Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an internet connection?**
Only for the first load. After that everything works offline; progress syncs
when you reconnect.

**Will I lose progress if I switch from guest to a real account?**
No. Sign out, then sign in properly — XP, streak and stars all carry over.

**Why can't I open Certifications?**
You're signed in as a guest. Sign out and sign in with Microsoft, Google or
email.

**Why am I not on the leaderboard?**
Guests are excluded, and so are players with no score. Sign in properly and
complete one level.

**Can I retake a level?**
Yes, as often as you like. Your best result is kept.

**Why does my score keep coming out at zero on some exam questions?**
They're probably multi-select. Every correct option must be ticked and no
incorrect ones — partial answers score zero, same as the real exam.

**Why do some credentials say "Coming soon"?**
Their question banks aren't written yet. 8 of 41 are playable today.

**Why don't I get reminders?**
Streak reminders are Android-only for now.

**Is my data private?**
Your answers and attempt history **never leave your device**. Only a small
public profile — name, XP, streak, badge count — is shared, and only so the
leaderboard can work.

## 11. Troubleshooting

**The app won't load**
Check your connection for the first load. Refresh. If it persists, clear the
site data and reload.

**My progress disappeared**
Progress is stored per device and per account. Check you're signed into the same
account. Guest progress lives only on that device and browser — using a private
window or clearing browser data will lose it.

**My score isn't on the leaderboard**
Confirm you're not a guest, that you've completed at least one level, and that
you're online. It may take a moment to appear.

**The exam timer ran out before I finished**
That's intended — the real exam does the same. Whatever you'd answered was
scored. Use the question palette to pace yourself next time.

**Videos don't play**
Some browsers block autoplay. The app falls back to a static image
automatically; nothing is broken.

**I'm on iPhone and there's no leaderboard**
iOS runs in local-only mode for now. Everything else works; your progress is
saved on the device.
